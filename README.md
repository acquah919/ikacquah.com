# Dr Isaac Kwesi Acquah — Academic Portfolio

Welcome to my personal academic website.

I am **Dr Isaac Kwesi Acquah**, a Medical Physicist, Lecturer, Researcher, and Consultant at the University of Education, Winneba.

I created this site as a central place to share my academic work, research interests, publications, teaching activities, and occasional reflections on physics, education, and professional practice.

**Website:** [https://www.ikacquah.com](https://www.ikacquah.com)

## About

My work sits at the intersection of **medical physics, imaging, radiation protection, and physics education**.

Over the years, I have been particularly interested in how physics can be applied to practical problems in healthcare and how complex scientific ideas can be communicated more effectively in the classroom.

Some of the areas represented on this website include:

- Medical Physics
- Medical Imaging
- MRI-only Radiotherapy
- Synthetic CT
- Artificial Intelligence in Medical Imaging
- Radiation Protection
- Physics Education
- Teaching and Learning
- Research and Publications

The site also includes a blog where I share thoughts from my teaching, research, and academic work.
